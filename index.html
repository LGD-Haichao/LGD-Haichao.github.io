02 / How I work

Three habits.

They are not slogans; they are what I actually do when a project is ambiguous.

01

Start with the decision

Who has to decide what, by when? That question determines which interviews, which data, and which document — not the other way around.

02

Write down the evidence chain

Every claim should point to a quote, a number, or a document. If I cannot trace it, I do not put it in the report.

03

Ship the smallest real thing

A working prototype with a known limitation is more useful than a beautiful plan with no failure modes.
