02 / How I work

How I try to work.

Not rules — just what I keep coming back to when a project is unclear.

01

Ask first, write later.

I would rather spend an extra day talking to people than write a confident answer to the wrong question.

02

Keep the receipts.

Every claim should have a quote, a number, or a document behind it. If I cannot trace it, I leave it out.

03

Make something that works.

A small working prototype is more useful than a beautiful plan with no failure modes.
